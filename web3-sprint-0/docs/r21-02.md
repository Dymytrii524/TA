# R21-02/B implementation

ADR-R21-02/B accepted by the product owner on 2026-09-29 22:10 EEST.
No Solidity or deployed contract changes. No merge or external deployment.

The reconciler verifies Funded/Settled/Withdrawn receipts, code/token/scope and
canonical finality before constructing a full ledger. Unknown verified escrows
remain UNATTRIBUTED with a reason and funding-event reference; they are not fake
TA deals. Missing proofs or TA mismatches are still failures.

F05-B/2 distinguishes total, TA and unattributed balances. Mixed and external-only
wallets retain authorized unsigned withdraw-all; allocations include exact TA
and external legs. API references from another company's historical binding are
redacted as RESTRICTED, with a separate subtotal; global amounts do not change.
Authorization is checked before reading and again before returning/preparing.

Migration 008 creates side-by-side immutable V2 snapshots and an atomic active
pointer. First activation replays ALL V1 snapshots against canonical anchors and
refuses mismatches. V1 rows/triggers are never rewritten. Late mapping of a
previously unattributed lot requires exact frozen terms and a privileged offline
approval row with two distinct users plus an evidence reference. It creates a new
projection revision, not a new payout. Runtime service roles MUST NOT receive
INSERT permission on wallet_mapping_approvals. No public approval endpoint exists.

The included approval table supports one initial controlled mapping per escrow.
Revoking/correcting a bad approved mapping requires a separate reviewed append-only
correction procedure; deleting or mutating history is intentionally forbidden.
Evidence-hash legitimacy and approver authority are responsibilities of that
trusted operational procedure, not proven by merely writing two UUIDs.

The pilot still replays full history. RPC calls are timed out, log ranges bounded
and retried without skips; capacity/timeout errors do not publish stale success.
This is not a scalable continuous indexer or protection against all resource DoS
or permissionless consumption of maxLiability. Production roles/auth adapter,
operational backfill approval, post-finality recovery and independent audit remain
separate gates.

Tests: `node tests/subsets.test.mjs` with local Anvil; repeat with dedicated local
WEB3_TEST_DATABASE_URL for real PostgreSQL reconciliation concurrency.
The suite covers the one-atomic outsider reproduction, mixed and external-only
wallets, late mapping after payout, malformed history, side-by-side backfill,
immutable audit, scoped response redaction and complete OpenAPI response validation.
